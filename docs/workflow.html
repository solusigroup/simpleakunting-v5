Hierarki Role & Permission

5 Level akses bertingkat
graph TB
    SU["🔑 Superuser"]
    AD["🛡️ Admin"]
    MN["📋 Manajer"]
    ST["👤 Staff"]
    KS["🧾 Kasir"]
    SU --> AD --> MN --> ST --> KS
    SU -.->|"Full Access + DB Mgmt"| ALL["Semua Modul"]
    AD -.->|"Master Data + Settings"| ADMIN_MOD["Master Data, Users, Roles, Cabang, Tutup Buku"]
    MN -.->|"Read Master + Approval"| MGR_MOD["Approval, Manufacturing, Agriculture, Import/Export"]
    ST -.->|"Transaksi Harian"| STAFF_MOD["Penjualan, Pembelian, Jurnal, Koperasi"]
    KS -.->|"POS + Laporan"| KASIR_MOD["Point of Sale, Laporan Dasar"]
RoleAkses Modul
SuperuserSemua modul + Database Management (truncate, fresh, drop, seed) + bypass role check
AdminMaster Data (CRUD Pelanggan, Pemasok, Persediaan, CoA), User & Role Management, Cabang, Tutup Buku, Aset Tetap
ManajerRead Master Data, Approval Workflow, Manufacturing, Agriculture, Import/Export, Laporan Koperasi
StaffTransaksi (Penjualan, Pembelian, Jurnal, Penerimaan, Pembayaran, Retur), Koperasi (Anggota, Simpanan, Pinjaman)
KasirPoint of Sale (POS), Laporan Dasar, Buku Besar
